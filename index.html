🎯 Client Challenge

A rapidly growing London-based FinTech platform was experiencing significant losses due to sophisticated fraud attacks that traditional rule-based systems failed to detect in real-time. Their existing fraud detection system had a 48-hour lag in identifying patterns, resulting in substantial financial losses and reputational damage. The client needed a solution that could:

  • Detect fraudulent transactions in real-time (<500ms latency)
  • Adapt to evolving fraud patterns without manual rule updates
  • Maintain full GDPR compliance and explainability for regulatory audits
  • Integrate seamlessly with their existing payment processing infrastructure
  • Scale to handle 50,000+ transactions per second during peak periods

💡 Data Valut AI Solution

Our team designed and deployed a cutting-edge, real-time predictive AI risk model leveraging advanced machine learning techniques and secure cloud infrastructure:

  • Ensemble ML Models: Implemented a combination of gradient boosting (XGBoost), neural networks, and anomaly detection algorithms trained on 2 years of historical transaction data
  • Real-Time Processing: Built on AWS serverless architecture with Lambda and Kinesis Data Streams for sub-500ms prediction latency
  • Explainable AI: Integrated SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to provide transparent, auditable risk scores for compliance
  • Continuous Learning: Automated model retraining pipeline that adapts to new fraud patterns weekly
  • GDPR-Compliant Architecture: Data anonymization, encryption at rest and in transit, and comprehensive audit logging
  • Secure Integration: RESTful API integration with existing payment gateway using OAuth 2.0 and TLS 1.3

Technology Stack: Python, TensorFlow, XGBoost, AWS Lambda, Kinesis, DynamoDB, API Gateway, CloudWatch

📊 Measurable Results

  • 30% Reduction in Fraud Detection Time: From 48 hours to real-time detection (average 320ms)
  • £2.4M Annual Fraud Losses Prevented: First-year ROI of 450% on project investment
  • 99.7% Model Accuracy: With a false positive rate of just 0.4%, significantly reducing customer friction
  • Zero Data Breaches: Full GDPR compliance maintained throughout deployment and operation
  • Scalability: Successfully processing 65,000 transactions per second during Black Friday peak
  • Regulatory Approval: Model explainability accepted by FCA for audit purposes

🎯 Client Challenge

A well-established UK retail chain with 150+ physical stores needed to modernize their aging e-commerce platform to compete with online-first competitors. Their monolithic PHP system was experiencing:

  • Frequent downtime during seasonal sales events (up to 6 hours per event)
  • Inability to handle more than 500 concurrent users without performance degradation
  • 12-week development cycles for new features due to tightly coupled codebase
  • No real-time inventory synchronization between stores and online channels
  • Poor mobile experience leading to 68% cart abandonment rate
  • Security vulnerabilities and no clear path to PCI DSS Level 1 compliance

💡 Data Valut AI Solution

We architected and delivered a modern, cloud-native microservices platform that maintained business continuity while enabling rapid innovation:

  • Microservices Architecture: Decomposed monolith into 12 independent services (Product Catalog, Inventory, Order Management, Payment, Customer, etc.) using Domain-Driven Design principles
  • High-Availability Infrastructure: Multi-region deployment on Azure with auto-scaling, load balancing, and 99.99% SLA
  • ERP Integration: Seamless integration with existing SAP ERP using Azure Service Bus and custom API middleware for real-time inventory sync
  • Modern Tech Stack: Backend services in Java (Spring Boot), event-driven communication via Kafka, React-based responsive frontend
  • DevOps Pipeline: Kubernetes orchestration, CI/CD with Azure DevOps, automated testing, and blue-green deployments
  • Security & Compliance: OAuth 2.0 authentication, API Gateway with rate limiting, PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment processing
  • Phased Migration: Strangler Fig pattern to gradually migrate from legacy system with zero-downtime cutover

Technology Stack: Java, Spring Boot, React, Kafka, Azure Kubernetes Service, Azure SQL, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SAP ERP Integration

📊 Measurable Results

  • 99.99% Platform Uptime: Zero downtime during peak Black Friday and Christmas sales periods
  • 5x Traffic Capacity: Successfully handled 2,500+ concurrent users with sub-2-second page load times
  • 40% Faster Checkout: Optimized checkout flow reduced cart abandonment to 32%
  • Real-Time Inventory: 100% accuracy in stock levels across all channels, reducing customer complaints by 55%
  • Development Velocity: Feature deployment cycle reduced from 12 weeks to 5 days
  • Revenue Impact: 85% increase in online sales year-over-year, £12M additional revenue in first year
  • PCI DSS Compliance: Achieved Level 1 certification within 4 months of launch
  • Mobile Experience: Mobile conversion rate improved by 120%

🎯 Client Challenge

A regional NHS Trust serving 1.2 million patients across multiple hospitals was struggling with fragmented patient data systems and mounting regulatory pressure. Key challenges included:

  • Patient data scattered across 15+ legacy systems (some dating back 20+ years)
  • Critical reports taking 4-6 hours to generate, delaying clinical decision-making
  • High risk of GDPR non-compliance with no centralized data governance
  • Inability to implement Trust-wide analytics or AI-driven clinical insights
  • On-premise infrastructure at end-of-life, with annual maintenance costs exceeding £1.2M
  • No disaster recovery plan meeting NHS Digital standards
  • Data breaches risk due to inadequate access controls and audit logging

💡 Data Valut AI Solution

We designed and implemented a secure, GDPR-compliant Data Vault on Google Cloud Platform, purpose-built for healthcare data governance and analytics:

  • Data Vault 2.0 Architecture: Implemented industry-leading Data Vault methodology for healthcare, providing full data lineage, auditability, and temporal tracking
  • Secure Cloud Migration: Migrated 15TB of patient data from legacy systems to GCP with zero data loss and minimal operational disruption
  • GDPR & NHS Compliance: Built-in data classification, pseudonymization, access controls, and comprehensive audit logging meeting NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit requirements
  • Healthcare Interoperability: HL7 FHIR integration layer enabling standardized data exchange with NHS Spine and external systems
  • Real-Time Analytics: BigQuery data warehouse with pre-built dashboards for clinical KPIs, operational metrics, and regulatory reporting
  • Disaster Recovery: Multi-region replication with RPO < 15 minutes and RTO < 4 hours, exceeding NHS standards
  • Data Governance Framework: Comprehensive policies, role-based access control (RBAC), and data stewardship processes

Technology Stack: Google Cloud Platform, BigQuery, Cloud SQL, Dataflow, Pub/Sub, Cloud IAM, Python, HL7 FHIR, Looker (BI)

📊 Measurable Results

  • 60% Faster Data Retrieval: Critical reports now generated in 90 minutes (down from 4-6 hours), accelerating clinical decision-making
  • 100% GDPR Compliance: Full audit trail, data subject access request (DSAR) processing time reduced from 30 days to 48 hours
  • £800K Annual Infrastructure Savings: Cloud-native architecture reduced maintenance and hardware refresh costs
  • Unified Patient View: Clinicians now access complete patient history from a single interface across all 15 previously siloed systems
  • Zero Downtime Migration: Completed migration with no service interruption or impact to patient care
  • NHS Digital Standards: Achieved NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit "Standards Met" status within 6 months
  • Analytics Capability: Enabled Trust-wide population health analytics, identifying high-risk patient cohorts for proactive intervention
  • Data Quality: Improved data completeness from 67% to 94% through automated validation and cleansing

"Data Valut AI didn't just migrate our data—they transformed how we manage and leverage patient information. The GDPR-compliant Data Vault has become the foundation for our digital transformation, and the 60% improvement in reporting speed has directly improved patient outcomes."

— Chief Data Officer, Regional NHS Healthcare Trust
